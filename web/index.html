    How this works
    • Search: your question is matched against 470 passages by meaning (vector search) and by words (BM25), and the two rankings are combined.
    • Answer: Claude Haiku 5.5 answers from the top five passages only. Every quote is checked against the passage it names before you see it.
    • Cache: a question that asks the same thing as an earlier one gets the stored answer, for free. "How did Caius Gracchus die?" never gets Tiberius's answer: names, ordinals and numbers must match before a cached answer is served.
    • Limits: the site allows 3 questions a minute and a small daily budget. After that, it answers only from the cache until the next day (UTC).

    Code, design decisions and measured results: github.com/zeusydev-lang/delphi-rag.

    The seven Lives: Cato the Elder, Cato the Younger, Tiberius Gracchus, Caius Gracchus, Caesar, Pompey and Crassus, in the Dryden translation revised by A. H. Clough (Project Gutenberg #674).
